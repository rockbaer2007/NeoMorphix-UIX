NeoMorphix UIX is a neumorphic theme for Home Assistant featuring soft shadows, rounded surfaces, and a tactile UI feel.

It includes 6 UIX variants across two styles:

- **Raised** (default neumorphic look): light, dark, and claude
- **Inset** (recessed elements): light-inset, dark-inset, and claude-inset

Requires [UIX](https://github.com/Lint-Free-Technology/uix) installed via HACS.
